# Key findings

Generated by `scripts/kpis.py` from the KPI query results.

1. **Delivery promises are padded.** 93.2% of 96,203 delivered orders arrived on or before the promised date, but customers were promised 24.3 days on average while the median delivery took 10.2 days (90th percentile 23.1).
2. **Late deliveries drive negative reviews.** Average rating falls from 4.29 (on time) to 2.27 (late). Late orders are 6.7% of reviewed orders but produce 32.6% of all 1-2 star reviews.
3. **Carrier transit is the main source of delay.** Late orders spent 27.9 days in carrier transit vs 8.0 for on-time orders, and 72.3% of late orders had been handed to the carrier on time. Orders where the seller missed the hand-over deadline were still 3.9x as likely to arrive late (21.1% vs 5.4%).
4. **Peak periods break the network.** On-time rate fell to 87.6% in 2017-11, 85.9% in 2018-02, 81.0% in 2018-03 against 93.2% for the whole period.
5. **Problems concentrate in a few lanes and states.** The largest below-average lane is SP -> RJ (8,257 orders, 86.0% on time). The weakest destination states are AL (78.5%) and MA (82.5%).
6. **A small seller group needs an improvement plan.** 42 of 424 sellers with 50+ orders fall below 85% on time or a 3.8 rating; they account for 8.2% of scorecard GMV.
7. **Late first orders reduce repeat purchases slightly.** Repeat purchase rate is 2.5% after a late first delivery vs 3.1% after an on-time one (repeat buying is rare on this marketplace overall).
